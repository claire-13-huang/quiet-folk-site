# Paper sound sources

Both recordings are CC0 1.0: reuse, modification and redistribution are permitted, including commercial use; no attribution is required. No music or watermark is added.

- `envelope-open.mp3`: [Envelope opened.WAV — Nickh69](https://freesound.org/people/Nickh69/sounds/472850/). Short 7.40–8.02 s excerpt from the publisher’s HQ preview, with edge fades, a gentle high-frequency rolloff and gain matching before quiet runtime playback.
- `paper-slide.mp3`: [paper slide.wav — semccab](https://freesound.org/people/semccab/sounds/154403/). Publisher’s HQ preview; natural 1.389 s paper movement, with edge fades and gain matching before quiet runtime playback.
